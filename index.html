The principle

Sound finds what light cannot reach.

01 — The source

Filtered through nine hundred metres of basalt.

  1. Pressure

    At 940 metres the water sits under ninety-four atmospheres. Nothing dissolved in it comes out again.

  2. Time

    It entered the rock as rain eleven thousand years ago and has been moving downward ever since.

  3. Mineral

    Basalt gives up silica, magnesium and a trace of lithium. We add nothing and remove nothing.
